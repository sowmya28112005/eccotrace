The Problem

E-Waste is the World's Fastest-Growing Waste Stream

Most people don't know how to dispose of electronics safely — leading to toxic chemicals polluting soil and water globally.

☠️

Toxic Chemical Leaching

Lead, mercury, and cadmium seep into groundwater from improperly discarded electronics, affecting millions.

🏭

Dangerous Informal Recycling

Workers burn e-waste to extract metals with no safety protection, inhaling toxic fumes daily.

📱

Short Device Lifespans

Average phones are replaced every 2–3 years. The upgrade cycle accelerates, the waste mountain grows.
